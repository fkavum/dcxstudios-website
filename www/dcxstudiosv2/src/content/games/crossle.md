Crossle isn't just a puzzle game. It's a daily ritual for your brain. Dive into infinite procedural levels and compete globally.

## About Crossle

DCX Studios' flagship game, Crossle is a word puzzle game built around a unique letter wheel mechanic. Each day brings a new challenge: spin the wheel, connect letters, and race to form the target word before time runs out.

Whether you're a casual player looking for a quick brain workout or a competitive gamer chasing the global leaderboard, Crossle has something for you.

## How to Play

1. **Spin the wheel** — letters are arranged in a circle around a shuffle button
2. **Connect letters** — drag from letter to letter to form words
3. **Form the target word** — match the correct sequence to win
4. **Compete daily** — each day brings a fresh procedurally-generated challenge

## Why Players Love Crossle

The game's elegant design keeps sessions short but satisfying. With a focus on daily challenges and global rankings, there's always a reason to come back. Simple enough to pick up instantly, deep enough to keep you hooked.
