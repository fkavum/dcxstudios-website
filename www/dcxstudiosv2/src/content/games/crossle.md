Crossle is an online real-time PvP word game — not a solo puzzle. Challenge a live opponent, build words across a shared crossword board, and outmaneuver them with strategy and vocabulary.

## About Crossle

DCX Studios' flagship game, Crossle is a competitive multiplayer word game built around real-time crossword duels. Two players share a board and race to place words, block each other, and deploy power-ups to swing momentum. Every match is different because your opponent is human.

Whether you're a casual player looking for a quick match or a competitive gamer grinding leaderboard ranks, Crossle delivers a fresh challenge every time.

## How to Play

1. **Match with an opponent** — get paired with a live player instantly
2. **Build words on the shared board** — crossword-style, competing for the same tiles
3. **Deploy power-ups** — use boosters to block, swap, or score big at the right moment
4. **Win the duel** — outscore your opponent before time runs out

## Why Players Love Crossle

Real opponents make every match unpredictable. The crossword format adds spatial strategy on top of vocabulary, so wins feel earned. A fast ranking system keeps matches competitive at every skill level.
